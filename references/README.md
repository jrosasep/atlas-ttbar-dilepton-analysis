# Reference provenance

Every number in `config/published_atlas.yaml` is transcribed from the ATLAS
paper source for arXiv:2311.07288, especially equations and text surrounding
the definition of `D`, Table 1, and the Results section.

Important methodological details retained in the project documentation:

- exactly one electron and one oppositely charged muon;
- at least two jets, at least one `b`-tagged;
- signal window `340 < m_ttbar < 380 GeV`;
- two validation windows, `380-500 GeV` and above `500 GeV`;
- the Ellipse method is the primary two-neutrino reconstruction;
- Neutrino Weighting is a fallback for about 5% of events;
- detector-to-particle calibration is simulation-based;
- the `D=-1/3` parton boundary is separately mapped to particle level.

Primary source:

> ATLAS Collaboration, Nature 633 (2024) 542-547,
> DOI:10.1038/s41586-024-07824-z, arXiv:2311.07288.
